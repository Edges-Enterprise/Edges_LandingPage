## 1a. How many percent of Nigerians have access to the internet?
Roughly 45% of actual, individual Nigerians have true internet access.
While official telecom statistics list over 150 million active subscriptions (which on paper looks like over 55% penetration), that metric counts SIM cards rather than actual human beings. Because most people buy multiple lines to alternate between MTN, Airtel, and Glo, the real number of uniquely connected individuals is much lower.
## 2. Is accessing the internet difficult for Nigerians?
No, accessing the internet is physically easy for anyone with a smartphone, but it is financially burdensome.
The reality of access today shows:

* Ubiquitous Network Signal: You can easily pick up stable data signals across most commercial areas, towns, and cities.
* Zero Physical Friction: Getting online does not require complex technical setups. SIM cards are practically free, cheap smartphones are everywhere, and once a bundle is loaded, streaming or browsing happens instantly.
